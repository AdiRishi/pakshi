---
title: Pakshi
description: An internal platform that lets non-technical teams build, launch and maintain websites without a developer in the loop.
sidebar:
  label: Overview
  icon: house
---

People describe what they want to an AI agent, which assembles pages from a curated library of well-designed blocks. Editors fix small things directly on the page. Every change happens in a draft that can be previewed and shared, and reaches production through the approval workflow its owners set.
